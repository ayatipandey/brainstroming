Scope: landing page (Persuade) at / and app screens (Operate) at /app. Audience: everyday cardholders and FIS judges. Action: Create account, Try the live demo. Proof: the working app itself, sourced figures, synthetic-model chart.

## Direction contract
seed: none (direction pinned by the brief; brand lock fixes palette and type)

THESIS: The product is the exhibit. The page is a white gallery wall that frames a real, working wallet, the way a museum frames one object, and refuses the fintech default of hero + three feature cards + testimonial carousel.

OWN-WORLD: White ground; warm greys (#f5f4f1 field, #e8e6e1 rule, #5c5a55 secondary ink); dark teal #012834 as the only dark field (stakes, wallet hero); green #4bcd3e only on actions; yellow #ffc845 only on the expiry clock. Atkinson Hyperlegible 700 display at magazine scale, -0.035em, left aligned; 400 body. Pill buttons, 20px UI fragments, 44px phone. No shadows at rest, no gradients, no glass.

STORY: Hero shows the wallet deciding. Stakes put the lost value on the clock. The engine scenes show sending and staying quiet. Features are framed one at a time. The comparison, accessibility, a day in the life, FAQ, and a plain close.

FIRST VIEWPORT: "Rewards that find you" at full display size, one sentence, Create account, and the live phone on the right with the 14-day clock and a nudge arriving.

SIGNATURE: The expiry clock drains as you scroll; the engine section pins and steps through scenes with a WebGL dissolve.

RISK: Heavy motion on phones; mitigated by lazy WebGL, transform-only travel, and reduced-motion paths.
